Three edits, three different costs: changing a value walks its downstream, adding a reference seeds one page, and editing prose walks nothing at all. A recompute-everything build system cannot tell these apart.
Dependency DAG
Teal is a definition, grey a reference. Amber edges are the ones the last propagation actually walked.
Why not 2-SAT
Detecting contradictions in prose needs claim extraction, and a wrong "you contradict yourself" is an accusation. With explicit names a contradiction is two definitions of one key — a hash collision. The useful half becomes trivial and the hard half disappears.
