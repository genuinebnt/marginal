Why these two

Outbox depth rising means events are being written but not published — search will silently drift from Postgres and history will develop a hole. Op-log lag rising means a consumer is falling behind the write path. Everything else on this page is context for one of those two numbers.

Two amber rows, and nobody is paged

diagnostics-service and assistant-service are degradable by design. When they fail the editor keeps working — you lose squiggles and suggestions, not writing. That is a deliberate architectural property, which is why they are amber here rather than red, and why neither one has a pager rotation.
