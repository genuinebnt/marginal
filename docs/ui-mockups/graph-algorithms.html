The sigil is drawn from those four numbers and nothing else — β₀ rings, β₁ lobes, edge density as the stroke. Two workspaces with the same shape get the same mark, which is the only reason it is worth showing.
Why β₁ twice
E − V + β₀ counts every independent loop, so a triangle of three pages that all cite each other scores as a hole. Fill the triangles and those vanish, leaving the loops that survive a local explanation. The second number is the interesting one; the gap between them is how much of the graph is just mutual linking.
